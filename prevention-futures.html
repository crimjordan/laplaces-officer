SOURCE DOCUMENTATION

Three evidence pathways, three different outcomes

The simulations deliberately do not treat opioid mortality, post-release recidivism, and early-childhood outcomes as interchangeable measures of crime. Each pathway uses a distinct outcome and evidence specification.

Observational association

Opioid treatment: methadone and overdose mortality

Larochelle and colleagues' Massachusetts cohort study of people surviving nonfatal overdose found methadone treatment associated with 59% lower subsequent opioid-overdose mortality over 12 months relative to no medication treatment. This page uses the 59% relative reduction only as an illustrative treatment-associated scenario parameter. It is not a causal estimate for expanding a community program.

Meta-analysis / odds ratio

Correctional education and recidivism

The 2013 RAND meta-analysis reported 43% lower odds of recidivism among correctional education participants. The model converts an odds ratio of 0.57 into a risk under the user-selected baseline: treated risk = (0.57 × baseline risk) / (1 − baseline risk + 0.57 × baseline risk). It does not incorrectly subtract 43 percentage points from the probability.

Randomized study / directional illustration

Perry Preschool and long-run justice outcomes

The Perry Preschool experiment found differences in several adult criminal justice outcomes, but not uniformly across measures. Because the available study summaries do not establish one transferable annual relative-risk reduction for a general population, this pathway starts with a user-editable illustrative 10% relative reduction. It is explicitly not a measured Perry Preschool effect size. Actual educational benefits and justice outcomes unfold over much longer horizons than this ten-year demonstration.

NIJ CrimeSolutions study profile
Interpretation: A research-informed simulator is not a forecasting model. Study populations, follow-up periods, selection bias, uncertainty, intervention fidelity, spillovers, and program costs all affect real-world transportability. Avoid using these outputs to estimate actual lives saved, arrests prevented, or return on investment.
