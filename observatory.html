Data & methods

Surveillance counts come from OpenStreetMap via ohsome, representing nodes tagged surveillance=* within approximate 20 × 20 km geographic boxes. Coverage and completeness vary across cities. Counts should not be interpreted as total installed cameras or as causal measures of crime control. Research metadata comes from OpenAlex and Crossref. All simulations are hypothetical.
