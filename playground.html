4. Uncertainty & the illusion of certainty

Each district is generated from the same underlying probability. Differences in observed rates are sampling noise, not differences in actual district risk.

About these experiments

All figures are generated in the browser using transparent formulas and synthetic values. They are designed to make methodological trade-offs visible, not to estimate real-world crime, camera effectiveness, deterrence, or algorithmic accuracy. No personal information or location data is used.
