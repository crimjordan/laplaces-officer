EXPERIMENT 03 / PARALLEL WORLDS

Two cities. One policy decision.

Compare hypothetical outcomes with and without an intervention. Change background trends and policy effects to see why a before/after comparison can mislead.

Baseline is 100 synthetic incidents in both worlds. The policy and shock are additive percentage-point changes relative to the shared baseline.

EXPERIMENT 04 / THE ORACLE'S PARADOX

When predictions change the future

A forecast may alter the outcome it predicts. Experiment with how successful prevention makes retrospective predictive accuracy difficult to interpret.

Synthetic cohort of 1,000; each person has a fixed seeded potential event draw and a second draw determining whether an intervention averts that event. No real person is represented.

Rules of the arcade

All simulations are intentionally simplified and entirely fictional. No location data, real personal records, or live policing predictions are used. Numerical results are consequences of stated assumptions, not empirical estimates. The arcade is designed to provoke methodological questions, not justify coercive decisions.
