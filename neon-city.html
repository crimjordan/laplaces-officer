Scientific transparency

64 invented districts, fixed synthetic baseline intensity, seeded event generation, and simplified detection/deterrence mechanisms. Patrols and towers alter the probability that an event is recorded. Patrols also reduce underlying activity when the deterrence control is above zero. The simulation is a conceptual teaching environment—not a validated crime model or a tool for operational decisions.
