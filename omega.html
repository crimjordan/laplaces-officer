MODEL CITIZENSHIP / EVIDENCE & METHODS

This is an experiment—not an oracle.

The 3D geometry, neighborhoods, populations, event counts, policy impacts, legitimacy dynamics, budgets and model coefficients are fictional. The engine uses reproducible random-number streams, seasonal intensities, recorded-versus-underlying event distinctions, policy-dependent patrol allocation, an intervention pipeline, and user-adjustable shock scenarios. It permits mechanistic thought experiments; it does not estimate the future of any actual city.

EMPIRICAL FINDING

Focused hot-spots policing

A Campbell review of 65 studies found small but meaningful crime reductions, with spatial diffusion of benefits more common than displacement. No universal effectiveness percentage is imported into OMEGA.

Braga et al., 2019
MECHANISM

Prediction feedback loops

Learning from police-discovered incidents can reinforce patrol concentration even when observed incident counts do not reveal the underlying distribution. OMEGA deliberately simulates that distinction.

Ensign et al., 2018 · Lum & Isaac, 2016
EMPIRICAL FINDING

Correctional education

A RAND meta-analysis reported 43% lower odds of recidivism among correctional-education participants. OMEGA's education pipeline is a stylized investment model, not an application of that odds ratio to citywide crime.

DOJ / RAND, 2013
EMPIRICAL CONTEXT

Treatment and overdose outcomes

The CDC reports medication treatment for opioid use disorder is associated with reduced overdose and all-cause mortality. Overdoses here are modeled as health emergencies, not offenses. Intervention coefficients are not treatment effect estimates.

CDC clinical overview

Model limitations: no fitted parameters, no empirical exposure denominators, no estimated uncertainty intervals, no validated resource-to-crime response function, no person-level data, no demographic profiling, no realistic policing legality assessment, and simplified spatial/temporal dependencies. The oracle option uses unobservable baseline conditions as a benchmark. Do not use OMEGA for real deployment or impact forecasts.
