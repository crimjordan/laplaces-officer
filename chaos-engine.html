Methodology & limits

Each node represents a fictional district connected to nearby districts plus a few longer network links. Disruptions may propagate probabilistically along edges. Patrol teams are allocated according to a selected rule and may reduce the persistence of disruptions. Surveillance changes the probability that disruptions are recorded; recorded signals influence the reactive policy. Randomness is seeded for reproducibility. No real cities, policing datasets, people, or camera locations are used.

The network and intervention mechanisms are invented for educational experimentation. This is not a calibrated crime model, a risk assessment tool, or a basis for deploying police.
