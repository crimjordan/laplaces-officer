06 / METHODS & COMPARISON LOGIC

What's being held constant?

Matched initial conditions

Each replication seed generates the same fictional residents and baseline districts across all experimental arms.

Fixed nominal budget

The amount is identical in each arm. Changing prevention share reallocates this amount between patrol and public services.

One mechanism at a time

Controlled comparisons change one configured factor. The factorial design deliberately changes three fully crossed factors to identify model interactions.

Multiple distinct outcomes

Compare underlying events, recorded events, arrests, mortality, recidivism, employment, treatment and program reach separately.

WHY THIS IS NOT CAUSAL IDENTIFICATION

Within-model treatment contrasts can be computed because the world-generating rules are explicit. But model assumptions, unknown transportability, confounding in source studies, uncalibrated rates and invented structural coefficients prevent interpreting these contrasts as real-world causal effects.
