3. Methods & transparency center

Surveillance atlas

OSM nodes matching surveillance tags, aggregated using the ohsome API for approximate 20 × 20 km boxes centered on selected cities. The count is neither a comprehensive inventory of installed cameras nor a municipal-boundary total.

Inspect source snapshot

Research discovery

Publication metadata from OpenAlex and Crossref. Search coverage depends on query design, indexing, and metadata completeness; the feed is not a systematic literature review.

Inspect research snapshot

Simulation assumptions

All districts, incidents, allocation decisions, and effects on detection are synthetic. No real-world police deployments, individual risk scores, or causal effect estimates are inferred.

Inspect interactive simulation

Global comparisons

Country-level comparisons should adjust for geographic coverage, mapping effort, population distribution, and inconsistent surveillance tagging. Global results remain unavailable until a reviewed dataset is integrated.

Interpretation checklist

QuestionInterpretation
Are missing counts zero?No. Unavailable data must be distinguished from an observed zero.
Does an OSM count equal installed cameras?No. Tagging and volunteer mapping are incomplete and variable.
Do changes in mapped counts equal installations?No. Mapping edits can create apparent growth without physical deployment.
Do simulations establish real-world effects?No. They illustrate mathematical mechanisms under specified assumptions.
Can the atlas guide enforcement?No. This is an educational and exploratory research tool.
