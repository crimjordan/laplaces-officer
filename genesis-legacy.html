What GENESIS does not claim

No geographic coordinates, names, identities, or personal histories represent actual individuals. It is a closed synthetic cohort, not a demographic forecast. No parameters are fitted to a real city's baseline incidence, crime reporting, policing exposure, employment, or education. Changes in observed counts do not prove policy effectiveness. A repeated-run distribution describes uncertainty given the invented model, not uncertainty over real-world causal effects. Policies here cannot establish reasonable suspicion or individual risk, and the interface must not be used for operational deployment.
